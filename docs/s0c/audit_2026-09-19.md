## Energy and mapping corrections

Reproduce with `python3 -m analysis.s0c_audit`; separate outputs are in
`results/s0c_audit/`. At 64 GS/s, the original stored CONS PCM/athermal/32-ring
cell is **2.0411×**, not the 1.25× in the reading/escalation. The 100 GS/s 3.1892×
uses 0.15 pJ/tap; using 0.05 with the same optical costs gives **1.0631×**.
Both comparator prices must be crossed against both physical corners. Increasing
digital cost is favorable to the claimed optical advantage, not conservative.
The three passing K cells share the same amplifier price, not independent
physical evidence of an advantage. The original kill gate is uninformative.

The athermal source reports **0.4 dB/cm**, Q 1.55e5, and 0.14 pm/K over 25–60 C:
[Qiu, Spring & Yokoyama, ACS Photonics (2015)](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/ph500450n).
It does not demonstrate those thermal properties on the registry's 0.051 dB/cm
platform with PCM actuators. The audit's 0.4-loss case is a sensitivity transplant,
not a claim that geometry/index/actuation can otherwise remain unchanged. Adding
all per-ring resonance losses assumes aligned resonances; the trained spectral
response, including couplers and packaging, is required for a link budget.

The arbitrary amplifier step at 3 dB is replaced **for this audit** by an explicit
input/output signal, required gain, ASE and receiver-noise sensitivity calculation.
Using linear noise factor F and gain G, two-polarization ASE is
`(F G - 1) h nu B`; this follows from the usual finite-gain noise-factor relation.
See [Taengnoi, Southampton thesis, §4.2](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/467317/1/Natsupa_Taengnoi_PhD_ForAward.pdf).
The script labels all input levels, NF, efficiency and limits as illustrative,
checks gain/output limits, and leaves total electrical power **unknown** when
idle power is unspecified. Signal power divided by in-band optical ASE plus an
assumed receiver noise is a simplified SNR proxy, not a BER prediction. Its power
lower bound omits out-of-band ASE and overhead; it cannot establish a product
advantage. No sourced co-integrated device budget is currently available.

Exact ring poles are `p=sqrt(1-K)*a*exp(i phi)` per **round trip**. At bare-SiN loss,
cavity-intensity FWHM for K=.05/.10/.20 is **.845/1.706/3.584 GHz**, using
`2 FSR/pi asin((1-|p|)/(2 sqrt(|p|)))`; the previous table was inconsistent with
its formula. Amplitude e-folding memory is **37.68/18.67/8.89 round trips**.
These are cavity linewidths, not a blanket statement about through-notch width.
At 64 GBd versus 100 GHz FSR, evaluate the continuous-frequency response at the
actual frequencies. A symbol-clock first-order recurrence or ZOH-CMT is not
validated across the whole band merely by limiting K to 10%.

An N-state IIR has an infinite impulse response. N poles do **not** imply at most
N useful FIR taps at every accuracy/rate; the stronger wording in PR-20b and the
roadmap is not a general theorem. This does not manufacture a positive PR-20
result: it means FIR equivalence must be measured for a defined channel, error,
bandwidth, noise and latency. PR-23-P reports only noiseless channel-specific fits.

## Pilot rationale and boundary

`PR-23-P` freezes a small local calculation before execution. For a known linear
channel C, ring response R, receiver scalar c and delay target T, Parseval gives
`mean |IFFT((c R C - T) X)|² = mean |c R C - T|²` for a unit-magnitude pilot
spectrum X and unitary FFT. With non-flat X, the corresponding response objective
is weighted by |X|². Gradients also agree. Renaming one side “task training” does
not distinguish methods when the observations and estimators are identical.

The preflight checks the exact model, BPTT ceiling, SPSA behavior, this identity,
and FIR-equivalent error. It is not a hardware-budget-matched comparison of
adaptive calibration with PAT. If the predicted equality is confirmed, the
registered decision is to stop paid expansion of this comparison. A future
experiment needs an explicit difference in observability, drift, nonlinearities
or measurement noise, a strong adapting baseline, and matched probes/writes.
The 8-ring/64-GBd preflight does not test the 32-ring/100-GS/s product cells.
